## Cultural note (inclusivity)
The provided dress shows an **Ethiopian Orthodox cross** motif on the hem (Christian-specific). For a
recurring EVERYDAY character meant to represent ALL Ethiopian kids (incl. Muslim families), prefer a
**neutral geometric tibeb** border for everyday/zoo scenes; reserve the cross-motif dress for Christian
holiday titles (Genna/Timket/Meskel). For a casual zoo trip, a simpler everyday habesha-inspired dress
reads better than full formal wear, while keeping her recognizable. Native reviewer to confirm.
