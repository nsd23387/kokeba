# Character Sheet — Mama / Emaye (Ethiopia) [supporting recurring character — visual lock]
> Eden's mother. Lock BEFORE scenes; attach as a reference image wherever she appears. Same family look.

## Mama (እማዬ, "Emaye") — warm Ethiopian woman, ~30s
### IDENTITY (LOCKED — never changes)
- Skin: warm brown (matches Eden — they read as family).
- Hair: natural dark curly hair, shoulder-length.
- Eyes: kind; gentle warm smile.
- Proportions: adult but soft, friendly storybook proportions; clearly taller/maternal next to Eden.
- Expressions on-model: warm smile, gentle/proud, calm & cozy (bedtime).
- Role: SECONDARY — warm, supportive presence; never overpowers the page or upstages Eden.

### WARDROBE (VARIES by scene; identity above stays locked)
- **Everyday / casual (DEFAULT — zoo, home, outing):** modern, comfortable everyday clothes a real Ethiopian mom
  would wear out — e.g., a simple blouse/top + skirt or trousers (or a relaxed casual dress) in warm colors that
  echo Eden's palette (cream / sage / gold accents) so they read as family. **No netela / no ceremonial dress**
  for casual scenes. Practical and warm, not formal.
- **Formal / cover / holiday:** a traditional **habesha kemis** with sage-green + gold **tibeb** trim and a light
  cream/sheer **netela** draped over the shoulders. Use for covers and holiday titles only.

## Reference image (REQUIRED)
- references/mama-ref.jpg — Ethiopian woman in habesha / with a netela (rights-cleared). Inspire, don't copy.

## Do / Don't
- DO keep the same face, curly hair, and family palette across pages; keep her secondary.
- DO dress her for the occasion (casual for the zoo); keep her IDENTITY locked even when the outfit changes.
- DON'T make her the focal point on animal pages; don't put her in formal/ceremonial dress for a casual outing.
