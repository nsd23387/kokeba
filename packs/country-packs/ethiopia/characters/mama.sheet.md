- Skin: warm brown (matches Eden — they read as family).
- Hair: natural dark curly hair, shoulder-length; often a light cream/sheer **netela** draped over the shoulders/head.
- Eyes: kind; gentle warm smile.
- Outfit: everyday habesha-inspired dress — cream with sage-green + gold **tibeb** trim at neckline & sleeves
  (echoes Eden's palette so they match as a family).
- Proportions: adult but soft, friendly storybook proportions; clearly taller/maternal next to Eden.
- Expressions on-model: warm smile, gentle/proud, calm & cozy (bedtime).
- Role: SECONDARY — warm, supportive presence; never overpowers the page or upstages Eden.
