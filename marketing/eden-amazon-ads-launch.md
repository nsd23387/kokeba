# Eden Goes to the Zoo — Amazon Ads Launch Plan (30-day test)

**Book:** Eden Goes to the Zoo (paperback) · ASIN **B0H56P7CMV** · $11.99 · royalty ~$2.99/book
**Goal:** a small, controlled 30-day test to learn what converts — buying *data + early reviews*, not profit.

---

## 0. Timing — don't spend yet (≈72h)
The corrected interior (bleed fix + Eden in pajamas) is still **"Updates in review."** The copy shipping right now is the pre-fix print. **Hold all paid spend until the corrected version is live**, so ad-driven buyers (and your first reviews) land on the fixed book. Use the wait for the pre-flight below.

## 1. Pre-flight checklist (before turning ads on)
- [ ] Corrected interior is **Live** (status no longer says "Updates in review").
- [ ] Listing is complete: cover, description, 2 categories, 7 keywords.
- [ ] **A+ Content** added in KDP (free, lifts conversion — do this).
- [ ] **2–3 honest early reviews** in place (newsletter readers, friends/family who actually read it). Ads convert poorly on a zero-review listing.
- [ ] Site "Buy on Amazon" live (done) + newsletter announcement queued.

## 2. Where / who
Amazon Ads via KDP: **Bookshelf → Marketing → Create campaign** (Sponsored Products), US marketplace.
*You run the spend — I plan and optimize, but I don't execute purchases or commit budget.*

## 3. Campaign structure (keep it simple — two campaigns)
1. **Auto — discovery.** Sponsored Products, *automatic* targeting. **$5/day**, default bid ~$0.35. Lets Amazon find search terms that match. After ~2 weeks, harvest the converting terms into the manual campaign and add poor ones as negatives.
2. **Manual — intent.** Sponsored Products, *manual* keyword targeting, **phrase + exact** match. **$5–10/day**. Keywords below.

(Skip Sponsored Brands for now — it wants a brand/3+ titles. Revisit when the Amharic alphabet book launches.)

## 4. Seed keywords (from our DataForSEO research)
Start as phrase + exact, bids **$0.30–0.50**:
- learn amharic
- amharic books for kids
- amharic book
- bilingual books for kids
- bilingual books for toddlers
- multicultural childrens books
- ethiopian childrens book

Also worth testing:
- amharic alphabet *(highest-volume pocket we found — browsers there may convert)*
- amharic for children · first words bilingual · habesha kids book · ethiopian gift for kids

Raise bids on keywords that sell; lower/pause keywords that spend with no sales.

## 5. Budget
~**$10/day total** ($5 auto + $5 manual) ≈ **$300 over 30 days**. Start at $5/day if cautious. This is a learning budget — expect a high ACOS early; you're paying for data and reviews.

## 6. Watch weekly
- **Impressions** (is it serving?), **CTR** (≥~0.3% is okay for books), **clicks**
- **ACOS** = ad spend ÷ ad sales. Early in a test, tolerate high ACOS; the goal is signal + reviews. Profitable ≈ ACOS under ~70% (royalty covers spend).
- **Conversion rate**, and **BSR / organic rank** movement (are ads lifting organic?)
- Add **negative keywords** for terms that spend with no sales (e.g., "amharic bible," anything off-audience).

## 7. Success criteria (then regroup)
- Any keywords converting at/under target ACOS?
- Did organic BSR/rank improve over the 30 days?
- Reviews accumulated? Newsletter signups from the site?
Decision at day 30: scale winners, cut losers, and decide whether book #2 (Amharic alphabet) is worth producing.

## 8. Free levers — do these alongside ads
- **Newsletter blast** (HubSpot) the day the clean version is live.
- **Ask early readers for honest reviews** (the single biggest conversion lever for a new book).
- **Short read-aloud clips** on Instagram/TikTok (the site has slots for both).
- **A+ Content** in KDP (free).
