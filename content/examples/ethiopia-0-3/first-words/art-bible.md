# Art Bible — First Words (shared across the series)

## Style
- Big, bold, flat shapes; high contrast for baby eyes. Minimal background clutter.
- Rounded, chunky forms. One object centered per page, filling most of the frame.
- Palette: navy #222B6D, gold #C9A227, white #FFFFFF, cream #E7D7A6. White is the breathing room.

## Characters (keep identical every page / every book)
- **Abeba** — baby/toddler girl: warm brown skin, big curious eyes, two small hair puffs, bright simple tunic, baby proportions (big head, small body).
- **Desta** — baby goat: cream/white, tiny budding horns, big friendly eyes, small gold bell on a collar. Sits in a corner of every page.

## Rules
- Abeba + Desta appear on every spread (the recurring 'find the goat').
- The word object is the hero; characters are secondary/corner.
- No text baked into the illustration (text is added in layout, so it can be localized).
