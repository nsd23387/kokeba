# Where's Desta?  —  ደስታ የት ነው?
**Kokeba · Ethiopia · ages 0–3 · English-led, Amharic words woven · framework: question + hide-and-seek + reunion**

> Refrain on every search page: **“Is Desta here? No!”** → ending **“There’s Desta!”**
> Participation: the baby finds Desta’s little **gold bell** peeking on every page, and says “No!” with you.
> ⚠️ Gate 1: native reviewer to confirm all Amharic (fidel + transliteration) and the goodnight phrase.
