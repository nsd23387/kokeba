# Eden Goes to the Zoo — per-spread scene prompts (gpt-image-2)
Attach **art/eden_ref.png** to EVERY prompt. On scenes with Mommy (cover, journey, final), ALSO attach **art/mama_ref.png**.
Settings: gpt-image-2, square **1024×1024** (1:1), white/clean background, generate 2–3, pick best.
NO text/letters in any image (the English + fidel are added at layout).

## Reusable STYLE block (prepend to every scene)
"Soft, warm children's storybook illustration — gentle painterly digital style, soft shading, subtle texture,
rounded friendly shapes, cozy warm lighting. Palette: cream/white, sage-green, gold, navy accents.
Character: Eden — EXACTLY as in the attached reference image (same face, warm brown skin, natural dark-brown
curly hair with a small gold star clip, white habesha dress with sage-green + gold geometric tibeb border,
baby/toddler proportions). Friendly, wholesome, ages 0–3. One clear subject, generous clean background,
NO text or letters in the image. Square composition.
CULTURAL & FORMALITY (must follow): inclusive of ALL Ethiopian kids (incl. Muslim) — neutral geometric tibeb on everyday outfits, NO Orthodox cross on everyday wear (holiday titles only); everyday (not formal) dress for this casual zoo trip. —"

## Scenes
- **Cover:** Eden standing at a colorful zoo entrance gate holding Mommy's hand, looking up with wonder; a few friendly animals peek over the gate. Leave calm space near the top for a title (no text in image).
- **Spread 1 — wake:** Eden waking and stretching with a sleepy happy smile in a cozy little bed, soft morning light through a window.
- **Spread 2 — journey:** Eden holding Mommy's hand, walking happily toward the zoo gate; Mommy is a warm Ethiopian woman, kept simple/secondary.
- **Spread 3 — lion (Anbessa):** a big friendly lion with a soft fluffy mane, gently roaring; little Eden in the corner cupping her hands to roar back, delighted. Lion large and cute, not scary.
- **Spread 4 — elephant (Zihon):** a happy elephant lifting its trunk high to wave; Eden waving back, big smile.
- **Spread 5 — monkey (Zinjero):** a playful monkey swinging on a branch, chattering; Eden giggling, hands up.
- **Spread 6 — giraffe (Qechne):** a tall gentle giraffe bending its long neck down to Eden, who stretches up on tiptoes to reach; sweet and warm.
- **Spread 7 — camel (Gimel):** a calm smiling one-humped camel; Eden bouncing/wiggling happily beside it.
- **Spread 8 — hippo (Gumare):** a round jolly hippo splashing in a little pool, water droplets; Eden laughing and pretending to splash.
- **Spread 9 — leopard (Nebir):** a gentle spotted leopard tiptoeing softly through leaves (friendly, not fierce); Eden tiptoeing quietly, finger to her lips.
- **Spread 10 — reflection:** Eden smiling and waving goodbye to all the animals as she leaves; the animals wave back in the background; happy and content.
- **Spread 11 — final/bus:** Eden sitting beside Mommy on a cozy bus seat, looking up at her with a sleepy happy smile; through the window the zoo and a soft evening sky fade away, a few first stars showing.
