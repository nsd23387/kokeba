**Dedication:** For our little star ★

**Spread 1**
- Left: Good morning, Eden, sleepyhead. / Time to wake and leave your bed.
- Right: Eden waking up and stretching in bed.

**Spread 2**
- Left: Shoes on feet, bow in hair. / Off to the zoo— we're almost there!
- Right: Eden holding Mommy's hand as they approach the zoo gates.

**Spread 3**
- Left: A lion stands strong and proud. / "ROAR!" says Eden, big and loud. / *Can you roar?*
- Right: Large lion illustration · አንበሳ · Anbessa

**Spread 4**
- Left: An elephant stomps down the way. / His trunk waves high to say, "Good day!" / *Can you wave?*
- Right: Large elephant illustration · ዝሆን · Zihon

**Spread 5**
- Left: A monkey swings from tree to tree. / "Ee-ee-ee!" chatters he. / *Can you chatter too?*
- Right: Large monkey illustration · ዝንጀሮ · Zinjero

**Spread 6**
- Left: A giraffe stretches to the sky. / Its long neck reaches way up high. / *Can you stretch tall?*
- Right: Large giraffe illustration · ቀጭኔ · Qechne

**Spread 7**
- Left: A camel walks with bumpity bump. / Wiggle and giggle— look at that hump! / *Can you bounce?*
- Right: Large camel illustration · ግመል · Gimel

**Spread 8**
- Left: A hippo splashes in the pool. / Splashing water is very cool! / *Can you splash?*
- Right: Large hippo illustration · ጉማሬ · Gumare

**Spread 9**
- Left: A leopard tiptoes soft and slow. / Where is it going? / Nobody knows. / *Can you tiptoe?*
- Right: Large leopard illustration · ነብር · Nebir

**Spread 10**
- Left: We saw animals, big and small. / Eden's favorite? / She loved them all!
- Right: Eden smiling and waving to all the animals as she leaves.

**Spread 11 — Final**
- Left: The bus rolls home. The sky turns blue. / Eden waves, "Bye-bye, zoo!" / She smiles at Mommy and starts to say, / "Can we come back another day?"
- Right: Eden beside Mommy on the bus, smiling up at her mother; the zoo fades softly into the distance.
