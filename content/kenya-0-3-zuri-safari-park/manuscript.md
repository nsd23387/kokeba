# Zuri Goes on Safari — 
**Kokeba · Ages 0–3 · Kenya**
Layout: LEFT = English story · RIGHT = illustration + Swahili word.

**Spread 1** — Good morning, Zuri, sleepyhead. / Time to wake and leave your bed.
**Spread 2** — Off we go to the safari park today!

**Spread 3**
- Left: A lion stands strong and proud. / "ROAR!" says Zuri, big and loud. / *Can you roar?*
- Right: lion illustration · simba · simba

**Spread 4**
- Left: An elephant stomps down the way. / Its trunk waves high to say, "Good day!" / *Can you wave?*
- Right: elephant illustration · tembo · tembo

**Spread 5**
- Left: A giraffe stretches to the sky. / Its long neck reaches way up high. / *Can you stretch tall?*
- Right: giraffe illustration · twiga · twiga

**Reflection** — We saw animals, big and small. / Zuri's favorite? Loved them all!
**Final** — The trip rolls home, the sky turns blue. / "Zuri waves, bye-bye, safari park!"
